Lessons from
the Road

Three things travel taught me about showing up.

Travel is a masterclass in adaptability. Plans change, flights get delayed, and the best moments are rarely on the itinerary.

1. Show up. Most opportunities come from simply being present. 2. Stay curious. Ask one more question than feels natural. 3. Adapt quickly. Flexibility beats a perfect plan every time.

These lessons apply well beyond the airport. They shape how I approach work, relationships, and growth.
