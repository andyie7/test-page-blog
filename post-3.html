Building Community,
One Game at a Time

What bringing people together has taught me about leadership.

Great communities aren't built by accident. They're built by people who consistently show up, make others feel welcome, and create space to connect.

Whether it's a team, a league, or a group of friends, the formula is surprisingly simple: be clear about the purpose, be generous with your time, and celebrate other people's wins.

I'll be sharing more stories about the communities I'm part of, and what I'm learning as I help them grow.
