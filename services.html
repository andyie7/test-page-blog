Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any experience before starting?

Absolutely not. I work with complete beginners through advanced athletes. Your program is built from where you are right now.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?

I ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations or reschedules at no penalty. Late cancellations (under 24 hrs) are charged 50% of the session fee.

Can I switch plans mid-month?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes — 10% off for first responders, military, and teachers. I also run seasonal promotions — sign up for the newsletter to stay in the loop.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients notice increased energy and strength within 2–3 weeks. Visible body composition changes typically begin at 4–6 weeks with consistent effort.
